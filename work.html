How We Build A Roof

From Tear-Off to Turnkey

01

Full Tear-Off & Deck Prep

We strip every roof down to bare decking and replace any damaged or rotted boards before a single shingle goes down. No covering up problems — no shortcuts.

02

Synthetic Underlayment

A full synthetic underlayment barrier goes down next, sealing every seam and fastener point before the shingles are ever installed.

03

Shingles, Installed Right

Dimensional shingles go down to manufacturer spec — proper nailing pattern, ridge ventilation, and flashing done right the first time.
