Metal Roofing

Built For The Long Haul

Standing-seam and metal panel systems for homeowners who want a roof that outlasts everything else on the house.

A Roof You Won't Think About Again

Metal roofing costs more up front than asphalt shingles, and it's worth being straight about that. What you get in return is a roof that holds up to hail, wind, and Texas heat far longer than a standard shingle roof, sheds water and debris better, and reflects heat instead of absorbing it — which can mean a real difference in cooling costs during the summer.

We install standing-seam and metal panel systems the same way we approach every roof: proper decking, correct underlayment, and installation to manufacturer spec — not just screwed down and called finished.
